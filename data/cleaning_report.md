Step 3 rule: 'tr' (trace) and empty cells are treated as missing. If any of the five amino acids is missing, the whole KFCT amino-acid profile for that food is replaced by the reference source (step 5), so profiles are never mixed within one food.

Step 4 formula: mg per g protein = mg amino acid per 100 g food / g protein per 100 g food. SAA (sulphur amino acids) = methionine + cystine.

Our user is a miller who buys grain, so farm-gate prices are the starting point for raw-material cost; the app lets the user type in their own prices.
